# v12 frozen flow-reuse and planning experiment

Two projects (DOM request/store/cache; React/Tailwind shared store), three arms
ordinary / raw-K1 / fs-K1, two fresh sessions per cell. Initial source and owner answers
are identical across arms. Raw and FS receive identical full learned bodies and index,
including the two source-reviewed migrated investigations. FS additionally has its
actual skills/MCP. All raw corpus files are readable, no special ranking hint.

Stage analysis: inspect existing flows, write analysis.md with cited relationships,
findings and material unknowns. FS also stores flows/findings and concise project
context through its normal tools; this extra cost is included.
Stage plan: add a new consumer or owner answer, start a fresh thread (no resume),
reuse prior artifacts, write plan.md. FS saves an unapproved revision linked to its
current flow/finding records. No product edits, installs, approvals or app execution.
Timeout 900 seconds/call, model gpt-6-sol medium, maximum 2 calls in parallel.

Primary checks are separate, not a weighted overall quality score:
1. Actual event→callback→callee→write/subscriber/error paths with exact evidence.
2. Preserve supplied input/retry/newer-draft/cleanup or style/variant/scope contracts.
3. Knowledge conditions and exclusions: no universal fetch/class/CVA prescriptions.
4. Distinguish known intent from unresolved intent; no repeated answered questions.
5. New consumer/answer changes invalidate affected analysis; preserve unrelated choices.
6. Plan covers specific deltas, verification and discretion; no silent scope extension.
7. Mechanism separately: persisted records, exact refs, freshness, routing, citations,
   no invented passing checks. Baselines aren't penalized for no FS record.

Manual semantic adjudication uses quotes from artifacts and frozen code/contracts.
The author is the evaluator; not blinded, no statistical generalization. Do not grade
by keyword counts or number of questions/rules. No predetermined architecture winner.
No model call is repeated unchanged just to win. A diagnosed fix gets a named new
cohort under the same fixtures/prompts/rubric; all failed usage remains counted. Provider
cache can't be reset. Record total and uncached-input+output separately. Timeouts with
unknown usage are unknown, never zero. Freeze inputs/runtime and probe cross-arm/repo
access before actual model work; fail rather than bypass isolation.
