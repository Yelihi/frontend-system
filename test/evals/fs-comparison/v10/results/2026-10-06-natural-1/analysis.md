## Qualitative review

Coordinator review of all six actual plans and delivered answers, not an independent quality score:

- All conditions identify domain or lifecycle ownership, propose narrow injected boundaries, preserve stated contracts, and leave material unanswered choices open. A short request did not make ordinary AI stop reasoning about architecture. None requires a class or named pattern merely to remove conditionals.
- Pricing: all separate pure exact USD calculation from transport, remove caller mutation, apply promotion before tax, round only the final total and keep the server authoritative. All leave the order wire contract and freshness unresolved. Ordinary and raw also ask about input precision/grammar. Raw and FS explicitly leave negative fixed promotion values open. FS supplies worked totals ($20 minus $5, then 10% tax = $16.50; $0.15 with 10% discount = $0.135, rounded to $0.14). These are useful concrete checks, but one plan's extra example is not repeatable overall superiority.
- Pricing controller: ordinary/raw propose guards against obsolete loads and binding the selected quote. FS leaves the exact overlapping-load and mismatched-ID behavior open after its explicit unknown answer. This shows different treatment of a boundary, not an established error: the plans are unapproved proposals and baseline guards have plausible correctness reasons. Do not mark every proposal as an unauthorized executed change.
- Extension host: all plan per-dashboard services, registration identity, idempotent cleanup, snapshot dispatch with live membership checks, synchronous nested emission, callback diagnostics and disposal suppression. All leave failed replacement and observable post-disposal behavior open. Ordinary/raw also discuss non-callback failures. FS records these as linked open decisions, including sink failure and public compatibility. Ordinary also independently asks about composition ownership; raw asks about command invocation.
- Raw notes did not turn unrelated source material into a mandatory plugin framework or pricing hierarchy. FS read the complete relevant primary bodies (`pricing-policy`, `extension-lifecycle`), each reached through an actual imported-call static candidate. Native evidence links and open-decision diagnostics are preserved. This establishes delivery and record linkage, not that reading the knowledge caused a better judgment.

## Actual response-fix exercise and remaining cost

Both FS plans requested `save_revision(detail: full)`. The corrected response included nonempty contract diagnostics. In pricing, the model explicitly read missing check-link diagnostics, added a planned `node --test` check, then made another save to improve examples. It did not execute tests or claim the proposed runner already existed. Open decisions and approval blockers remain. Thus the repaired full-save path was exercised by a real model, beyond deterministic regression coverage; this is not evidence of lower cost or a passing implementation.

FS used 9.97× ordinary total tokens for pricing and 4.58× for the extension host. Uncached input plus output ratios were 2.20× and 2.81× respectively. Raw was cheaper than FS too. Cache reuse, different questions and tool trajectories preclude attributing these numbers to routing alone. No general or repeated FS design-quality/token advantage is established. The reliable observed addition is structured, inspectable decision/knowledge/contract linkage with retained approval gates.

## Measurement limitations and recovery

These are two reused five-module authored projects, one model and one journey per arm; notes are synthetic, not the full real knowledge corpus. The coordinator authored fixtures, mapped answers and reviewed outcomes. There is no blinded statistical superiority claim or generated implementation test. The earlier anonymous review is in development-1 and is not a grade for these plans.

Four initial question calls completed successfully but the evaluator assumed optional evidence was always a `{path, quote}` object. The parser repair recovered those original outputs/usage without model reruns. Two calls not yet started were then run once. The parser-only manifest amendment and original frozen harness are retained; prose is not counted as verified quotation.
