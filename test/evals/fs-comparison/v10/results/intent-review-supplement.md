# User-requested structural intent review

Added during holdout question collection, after the user supplied an illustrative central edit dispatcher versus operation-method API. The example is NOT added to the knowledge corpus and is NOT a class/OCP rule. Existing generation prompts and primary outcomes remain frozen.

Additional qualitative observations, not retrospective points:

- Does a proposed boundary follow actual callers, ownership and a concrete planned variation?
- Does the plan distinguish observed behavior, inferred purpose and unknown owner intent?
- Does it compare a small structural change with keeping the existing shape, including real tradeoffs?
- Are methods/classes/interfaces/patterns justified by responsibilities rather than their names or the removal of an if statement?
- Are existing behavior and state lifetime preserved by the proposed structure, including repeated operations and failures?

Apply to all conditions. On these fixtures, relevant evidence includes independent panes versus repository coordination, pure pricing policy versus transport, request versus widget state, queue versus workspace credentials, and dashboard versus extension registrations. The suite does not yet test a central dispatcher versus a replayable command architecture. Do not claim it proves that example. Structural observations do not replace original answer-consistency and actionable-check outcomes.
